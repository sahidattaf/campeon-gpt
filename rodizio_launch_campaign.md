# Bossa Asado i Mar – Rodizio Skewers Night Campaign

This plan outlines a social media campaign to launch our new Brazilian rodizio skewers night.

## 1. Instagram Reels Ideas
1. **Behind the Grill** – Showcase chefs preparing sizzling skewers over open flames, with quick cuts of the cooking process.
2. **Skewer Sizzle Montage** – Close-up shots of different skewers hitting the grill, highlighting the sound and smoke.
3. **Guest Reactions** – Capture diners tasting the skewers for the first time and sharing quick testimonials.

## 2. Sample Ad Copy for Facebook & Instagram
"Join us for *Rodizio Nights* at Bossa Asado i Mar! Enjoy all-you-can-eat picanha and coastal vibes every Friday. Book your table now and bring your appetite."

## 3. Recommended Hashtags
- #RodizioNights
- #CoastalAsado
- #BossaFlavors
- #SkewersOnTheSea
- #AllYouCanEatPicanha

## 4. Suggested Timeline
- **Two Weeks Before Launch** – Teaser posts introducing the concept and setting the mood.
- **Launch Week** – Daily Instagram Stories showing prep, guest excitement, and live grilling.
- **Post Launch** – Follow-up posts with customer testimonials and highlights from the first weekend.

# Bossa Asado i Mar – Quarterly Staff Performance Review

Use this template to evaluate both kitchen and front-of-house staff. Replace the guidance with specific details for each employee.

## 1. Technical Skills
- Proper grilling techniques / seafood handling
- Accuracy of orders and preparation
- Knowledge of menu items and specials

## 2. Speed and Efficiency During Service
- Timely ticket completion
- Ability to keep up with high-volume periods
- Consistency in serving times

## 3. Adherence to Safety and Sanitation Protocols
- Correct use of food handling guidelines
- Cleanliness of work station
- Compliance with local health regulations

## 4. Teamwork and Communication
- Coordination with other staff members
- Positive attitude and collaboration
- Clarity in communication with the team

## 5. Suggestions for Improvement
- Identify areas where the employee can grow
- Provide specific action items or training goals

## 6. Goals for Next Quarter
- Measurable objectives for the upcoming quarter
- Set milestones for skill development and service quality
